How to play

  • Drag or flick the ball to aim and throw.
  • Or press and hold to power up, then let go.
  • Grab bones and stars. Unlock new parks and pups with your total points!
  • Sometimes the next ball is special: a golden ball doubles the catch, and a bouncy ball keeps hopping.
  • String mid-air catches for combos. A big combo makes your pup flip!
  • Collect bones to earn Treats. Spend them in Dress Up to fancy up your pup. Treats never spend your Total points.
